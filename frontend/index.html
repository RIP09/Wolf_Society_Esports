Our Story

The Underdog Revolution

Wolf Society Esports was born from a simple belief: that passion, strategy, and heart matter more than money or reputation. We are India's premier underdog esports organization, competing across BGMI, Valorant, and MOBA 5v5 with a hunger to prove that underdogs can conquer the world.

Our flagship team, Divine Wolf, represents the pinnacle of our competitive spirit. We started as a group of passionate players who refused to be overlooked. Today, we stand as a professional esports organization with a growing fanbase, multiple tournament wins, and a roster of elite players who share one vision: to dominate the Indian esports scene.
